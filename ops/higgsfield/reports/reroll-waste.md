## What counts as waste, and what is normal

A **shot** is one planned clip or image. We count a new generation as a **repeat** of a shot when, within 24 hours, it uses:
- the **same start image** with the **same model** and the same spoken line, or
- the **same reference images** with an **almost identical prompt** (85%+ the same words).

The first try of every shot is **normal** and is not counted. Every extra try is counted as repeat spend. We split repeats into three kinds:

| Kind of extra try | What it means | Is it waste? | Last 7 days: tries | Credits | Dollars |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Blind retry** | Same prompt, same settings, just pressed "generate" again | **Yes. Clear waste.** The editor hoped for luck instead of fixing the prompt. | 110 | 1,318 | $66 |
| **Edited retry** | The prompt was changed between tries | Partly normal. Fixing a prompt is part of the job, but it is expensive on video. | 106 | 1,569 | $78 |
| **Made several at once** | 2–4 copies of the same image or clip made at the same second | Mostly waste for video. Fine for cheap images if someone needs options. | 232 | 498 | $25 |

| Deleted later (unknown) | A charge with no clip left in the history | Unknown. Probably mostly rejected tries. | 1,225 charges | 6,299 | $315 |

**"Same mistake" rerolls:** 35 shots in the last 7 days (126 in 25 days) were tried **3 or more times with the prompt almost unchanged**. In 25 days those unchanged extra tries cost 2,801 credits (about $140).

Videos are where the money is: in the last 7 days, video repeats were 95 tries but 2,705 credits (80% of all repeat credits). Image repeats were many tries but cheap (1.5–3.5 credits each).

10 of the 10 most expensive repeats this week are Seedance 2.5 videos. Each of these shots wasted 98 to 189 credits on extra tries.

## 5 fixes

1. **Approve the still image first, then animate once.** A still costs 1.5–2 credits. A Kling clip costs 6–25. A Seedance clip costs 28–210. Most video retries come from a start frame or story that was not right. Fix it at the image stage.
2. **Two tries per shot, at most.** A 3rd try needs a changed prompt and a lead's OK. Blind retries (same prompt again) cost 1,318 credits ($66) last week alone.
3. **Treat Seedance 2.5 as "final render only".** Seedance costs about 7 credits per second (a 15-second clip is 105 credits). Test the motion and timing in a short Kling draft first (5 seconds, std, sound off is about 6 credits). Then make one Seedance render.
4. **Set "number of results" to 1, and never submit the same video 2–4 times at once.** Making copies at the same second cost 498 credits last week and about 3,187 credits over 25 days, mostly duplicate Kling videos.
5. **Draft short and silent, finish once.** In our charges, Kling costs about 1.25 credits per second in std with sound off, 1.75 in std with sound, and 2 in pro with sound. So std alone saves little. The big savings are **sound off and short length** for drafts (a 5-second silent std draft is about 6 credits; a 15-second pro clip with sound is about 30). Switch to pro, full length and sound only for the approved take. Also: **put the brief ID at the top of every prompt and stop deleting rejected tries** (or log them). Then the next report can name the brief for each credit, and the 21% of spend that now disappears becomes visible.

## Gaps and limits

- **History reached back only to 2026-09-19, not the full 30 days.** We read 60 pages (6,000 generations, the limit we set). Sep 14–18 is missing.
- **Exact charges cover only 2026-10-07 01:58 UTC to 2026-10-14 04:03 UTC** (3,600 charge and refund lines). Before that, credits are estimates from the same settings (the typical charge for the same model, mode, length and sound) or the price list.
- **Deleted clips are invisible.** 1,225 charges (6,299 credits) in the last 7 days have no clip left. We cannot group them into shots, so they are **not** in the repeat numbers. The real repeat waste is probably higher than 11%.
- **Grouping is by matching images and text.** A few shots may be joined or split by mistake. If an editor made a new start image and then a new video, that is not counted as a repeat.
- **Names:** Higgsfield does not record who made a clip. We name an editor only when the spoken lines match one brief with confidence. Everything else is "unknown".
- **Failed and blocked jobs are left out.** They were refunded (2,245 credits in 7 days), so they cost nothing.
