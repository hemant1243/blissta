# Emotion and belief map (from our own winners, Oct 2026)

Built from 01_WINNERS, the Corvael VOC counts (30,888 comments) and 40+ Corvael concept briefs in Drive.

## The words, simply

| Word | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar | WHO: one person in one situation | A man of 55–72 who bought nattokinase 3 times and felt nothing |
| Desire | What he wants | To stop worrying about what is building inside him |
| Belief | What he thinks is true now, and what stops him buying | "Nattokinase doesn't work on my body" |
| Emotion | What he feels the second the ad hits. It makes him stop and care | Anger: "I got cheated" |
| Angle | The door you walk in through | The fake bottle, the dose, the label |
| Hook | The first line, which opens that door | "Your wife paid forty dollars for it." |
| Villain / mechanism | The "why" that makes the new belief make sense | Fibrin; a fifth of a dose |

**An ad** is an avatar, plus an emotion to stop him, plus a belief shift to move him, plus proof he can check, plus the offer.

Most winners use **two emotions in a row**:
- **The opening emotion** stops the scroll: anger, fear or recognition.
- **The closing emotion** makes the sale: relief, hope or control.

Fear on its own makes people freeze or scroll away. Fear followed by a way out makes them buy.

## Our proven winners, decoded

| Winner | Avatar | Opening emotion | Old belief | New belief | Closing emotion | Proof he can check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sumo (Corvael) | Natto buyers, and their wives | Anger at being cheated | "Natto is natto" | "Most of it is fake, and I got played" | Control | Flip the bottle and count |
| Six Weeks (Corvael) | Took it, felt nothing | Recognition ("that's me") | "My body doesn't respond" | "It wasn't my body, it was the dose" | Relief | Read the FU on the label |
| China MB ($4M+) | MB users who fear fakes | Fear of poisoning myself, plus anger at being scammed | "MB is MB" | "Mine could be industrial junk" | Control, then trust | Blue pee for 14 hours, the water test |
| Pain Nurse (PEA) | Gabapentin users | Hope from an insider | "Gabapentin is my only option" | "There's another path that people who know use" | Hope | "Ask why she chose it" |

**The common core:** the man already tried something and it failed. Deep down he blames himself ("I'm too old, my body is broken"), which is **shame and defeat**. The winners **move the blame away from him**, onto the bottle, the dose or the fake. That turns shame into anger, then relief ("it's not hopeless"), and the test gives him control.

The Corvael VOC doc calls this the money room: **"BLAME REDIRECTED. It wasn't you, it was the dose."**

## Corvael: one emotion or many?

The 40+ concepts spread across about 6 different belief shifts. **Only 2 of them are proven.**

| # | Belief to shift | Old → new | Emotion | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | "It doesn't work on me" | → it was the dose or form, not me | Shame → relief | **PROVEN** (Six Weeks) |
| B | "All natto is the same" | → most is fake or underdosed, and I can check | Anger → control | **PROVEN** (Sumo) |
| C | "I do everything right (statin, diet), so I'm safe" | → those don't touch fibrin | Unease and fear | Untested (Man Who Did Everything Right, Statin vs Removal) |
| D | "Cold hands and the 2am cramp are just age" | → these are signs | Recognition → concern | Untested (Fibrin Symptoms, Relay) |
| E | "Stroke is sudden" (family history) | → it builds for 20 years | Fear and grief | Untested, and near the compliance line (no "stroke prevention") |
| F | "It's too expensive" | → it's cheaper than 5 separate bottles | Smart-buyer pride | Untested (Comment Reply Price) |

The Tier List got eyes with curiosity but had no emotional payoff, so it had a high hook rate and a bad CPA.

The BP-numbers ad failed because the number (under 150) created no real fear: "140 is perfectly fine".

## Rule from now on

Tag every ad **AVATAR · OPENING EMOTION · BELIEF SHIFT (A–F) · CLOSING EMOTION**, and put the belief letter in the ad name.

Read the results **by belief**, not just by concept. After 2–3 batches we'll know which belief is the money, instead of guessing.
