## 0. READ FIRST: the rules every static has to pass

**The two lines (00_COMPLIANCE, 00_START_HERE):**
1. Never claim it cures, treats, prevents or reverses a disease.
2. Never claim it replaces a medication. Never tell anyone to stop, reduce or change a prescription.
"Everything else is approved. Do not ask permission." Don't hedge an approved claim down to "may support": "A brand that hedges every sentence reads like it doesn't believe its own product."

**Also always:**
- Never overclaim how fast or how big the result is. No day-one transformations.
- Never write "talk to your doctor". That exact phrase has had pages banned. If a safety note is needed, the narrator says it as a conversation with "whoever prescribes for them".
- Never invent a number. Write [BRACKET] and flag it. ("11,000 bottles sold" was invented and caught.)
- Never invent a study, a quote or a named person. Never improve a real quote.
- No geography: no "Made in USA", no Utah, no state or country. Use: cGMP certified facility · NSF verified · third party tested · COA available per batch · pharmaceutical grade / USP grade. (Hemant lifted the ban for the MB gummy batch on 27 Aug 2026 only. Products are made in Dongguan, Guangdong. 114 comments in one month say "ships from China".)
- One guarantee number per asset. **60 days store-wide.** The Corvael 120-day promo "ends September 2026", so it should be over by now ⚠️ (confirm before using 120).
- Free shipping applies to bundle 3 only. Bundles 1 and 2 are always $4.99. Never write "free shipping on every order".
- Product name is **Corvael**, not Corbel (forced by a trademark squatter). Older files still say Corbel.
- The ad's price, discount and guarantee must match the landing page and cart exactly: "This buyer notices a mismatch and then posts about it."

**Meta specific:**
- Keep out of primary text: prescription drug names, celebrity/public figure names, prescription interactions. (All allowed in advertorial, email, VSL.)
- Avoid condition names in static headlines.
- "Suppressed / banned / hidden" get flagged as conspiracy. Use "underused" or "overlooked".
- Never show real prescription drug labels or real competitor labels.
- No persona that looks like a real public figure. Flag any AI avatar whose voice or accent drifts between cuts.

- PAI-1 rises decade after decade after 50.

## 1. BRAND

### Positioning
- **Brand-level villain: ENDOGENOUS COLLAPSE.** "Your body stopped making this." Every Blissta product refills something the body stopped producing: MB refills the cell's power supply, PEA refills the nerve's ceasefire signal, Aeon refills the GHK-Cu repair signal, and Corvael refills the fibrin-clearing system that "slows down after 50".
- One villain per ad. Never mix the villains:

| Product | Locked villain |
|---|---|
| MB | Electron leak |
| PEA | Nerve starvation |
| Corvael | Fibrin decline |

- **The mechanisms Blissta owns:** (1) **Fibrin decline**: "Mainly used by us." (2) **The five-link chain**: five ingredients, five jobs, break one and it stops. "We created it. Nobody else in the market is saying it." Rule: go deeper into a proven angle; don't invent new ones. "It's a new door into a proven angle."
- **Market sophistication:** the whole category writes at level 3 (it names a mechanism). Blissta writes at level 4: a mechanism nobody else has named, or a new way into a known one. Level 5 (identity) is the next move. "A concept that just states a mechanism is not a concept."
- **The pattern across all three brands:** "All three brands' best rooms are 'I bought this category and got burned.'" (MB fake MB = $4M+; Corvael Sumo = fake natto; PEA's strongest unbuilt rooms = tried PEA and felt nothing / bought cheap Amazon PEA.)
- **Trust is the product.** Category competitors run fake doctors, deepfakes and fake Elon endorsements (Arialief "Dr. Richard Moore", Nerve Calm, Nerve Freedom "15-second pepper trick"). "Every real trust signal we show is a conversion advantage."

### Promise / offer (02_THE_OFFER, 00_BLISSTA_FACTS)
- **Guarantee: 60 days. Every SKU. Keep the bottle. Refund within 48 hours. No return required.**
- Risk reversal lines that work:
  > "We buy it back."
  > "We eat the cost."
  > "Send back the empties and every dollar comes home."
- **The winning offer:** "Up to 71% off + four free gifts" (gifts worth $81 to $85). This is the default and the proven winner. Same offer, other wordings: "Buy 3 get 2 free", "Five bottles for the price of three", or a price anchor. The 71% includes the 10% email popup. Without it, say ~61%. Always push the big bundle. Different products have different offers, so check per product.
  > "That was the same offer and it always performed better than everyone, rather than saying buy three get two free." (Hemant)
- Tiers on every product: Starter (1 bottle/30 days), **Most Popular (3/90, center, gold badge, default)**, Best Value (5-6/150). Always show day-supply framing, daily cost and % saved.
- Subscription is on by default (~25% off the entry tier). Checkout is currently subscription-only (⚠️ this conflicts with the brand's own rule that a one-time default builds trust and forced auto-subscribe is on this buyer's instant-distrust list).
- **Never:** a countdown that resets. "You'll kick yourself." Manufactured scarcity. Scarcity has to be external and true: a batch, a production run, an allocation.
- Live lies to avoid repeating: "free shipping on every order"; the PayPal logo at checkout (PayPal doesn't work); $87.99 in ads for Corvael (the page says $97.99); 120/60/30 in the same ad.
  > "Your ad says guaranteed for 120 days in one place, 60 days in another and 30 days in another. So which is it?" (customer)

### Voice (01_VOICE_OF_CUSTOMER_SOP, 01_CONCEPT_AND_COPY_SOP, 04_CREATIVE_STRATEGY_SOP)
- **"We do not write in our own words. We write in his."** "AI analyses. AI never generates." No invented or improved quotes.
- **Transcribe, don't translate.** Match profanity exactly ("If they swear the ad swears"). Keep filler (you know, I mean, honestly, look). Keep broken sentences and repetition:
  > "Six years. Six years I took every pill they gave me."
  > "I just. I don't know what else to do."
  "If you improved a line, you damaged it." "If a customer said 'it did nothing,' do not write 'it proved ineffective.'"
- **Words they use:** clogged arteries · still clogged · my calcium score · cold hands · morning wood · did some digging · connect the dots · not USP grade · would not trust supplements from China · so sick of them blaming everything on age · I've tried everything and nothing's moving · fish tank grade · wake up tired · dragging · fog · the 2pm wall · running on fumes · I want myself back
- **Words they never use (banned in copy):** cardiovascular health · vitality · optimise · wellness journey · discover · transform · breakthrough · unlock · elevate · delve · game changing · life saver · worth every penny · changed my life · Doctor Approved
- **Four laws in every concept and every winner:**
  1. **Explicit absolution.** It wasn't him. Blame lands on the bottle, the dose, the missing link or the measurement. Never on him, and never on the ingredient he already believes in.
  2. **Concession before the argument.** "Day one, nothing happens." "Garlic is genuinely good for you." "In a market where everybody overclaims, the only believable person is the one who tells you what won't happen."
  3. **Something he verifies himself.** Free, immediate, before the offer.
  4. **The same short check set, same words, every time**, until he repeats it to his own doctor.
- **Hooks:** six hooks per concept means six different people walking in, not six wordings. Never repeat a construction. Every hook needs a specific (a number, name, date or scene). No pronoun without a referent. Scenes beat statements. "If a rattled 60-year-old wouldn't say it, cut it." "The hook's only job is to get their eyes."
- **A "cost" is a filmable scene**: one concrete action, one other person in the room, one moment in time. "He handed me a number. Twenty-eight pounds." Not "struggling with stubborn weight". "If you can't put a camera on it... it's a benefit statement with a sad face."
- **Villain axis:** the category is stuck on the romantic partner as villain. Openings nobody has used: the child, a stranger (first "ma'am" from a 22-year-old cashier, the seat-belt extender), the job, your own past self ("I used to carry a fridge up two flights. Now I can't tie my boots."), the sibling, the peer group/reunion, absent kids, the refusal (saying no to the doctor), the numbers ("47 matches at 32. 3 last month at 47."), the widower, the shift worker, the professional's face changing at the scale.
- **Worn-out openings (don't write):** ex/boyfriend ultimatum, menopause weight, cheated on with a younger woman, alcohol/wine confessional, kids commenting on appearance, doctor testimonial, wedding photo anxiety, foamy urine, "do not buy [product]" reverse hook, public apology reversal, fake-product warnings, "sale ends today".
- **Enemy:** "Never a person, never the reader." Exception: PEA post-surgical. Never attack the surgeon. "Keep your surgeon. This is for the part he told you he couldn't fix."
- **Pre-brief** (one sentence each): HOW (voice: friend, confessor, insider or witness) · WHEN (the 30 seconds where the pain peaks) · WHO (which belief leads) · ENEMY · WHAT (format, decided LAST). Then a one-sentence hypothesis. "No hypothesis, no budget."
- **Anti-repetition gate:** write MECHANISM / PERSONA / STORY / REGISTER. If two concepts share two of the four, change one. Max 5 concepts per session.
- **Tone with the reader:** "We know how this sounds" disarms him. Acknowledge his skepticism out loud. Proof before promise. "Emotion opens the door, data makes the sale." (On MB, pure-feeling identity-loss ads ran 15-20% hook and 0-1 sales.)
- **Persona rule:** clone a proven persona (BlisstaTopWin channel) and keep his pacing and voice. Don't invent one. Practitioner voice FAILED in cold prospecting (warm/retargeting only) ⚠️, yet the best PEA asset is a pain nurse; nobody has reconciled this.
- Internal writing to Hemant: answer first, plain text, **no em dashes**, no cheerleading, say what's wrong.

### Static-specific learnings
- MB static ROAS 0.72x vs video 0.92x. 90% of the top 20 MB ads are video. "Static validates, native scales": validate an angle on static first, then write the native. 4+ natives on unvalidated angles got $0 spend.
- PEA statics that work: **"4 Reasons Italian Doctors Reach For This" ~$31 CPA**. Mechanism reframe statics ~$60 CPA. 9 Painsomnia statics built, never used in video. 27 PEA static concepts × 2 core angles built.
- MB: 45 gummy static concepts. Audit: 24 pass, 8 fix, 13 kill.
- Long-copy static advertorial carries **argument, not narrative**: a first-person male "Reddit post" voice, "I tried everything and none of it worked", angry-friend rant, label positioning ("everything on the label, nothing to decode"). It kills motion hooks and visual reveals. "Statics don't have a reveal. They have an argument."
- Testing statics: lock the concept, visual and background; change only the headline. Mix: 70% variations of winners / 20% one-variable iterations / 10% wild cards. Judge problem-aware ads on hook/hold, solution-aware on hold/CTR, most-aware on CPA/ROAS. Kill at $150 with no sale. CPA ceiling $100. Break-even ROAS 1.2.
- Hook rate under 25% is dead. Zone 3-4 emotional intensity (fear, anger, disgust, shame) gets 49-80% hook; Zone 1-2 soft hooks get 17-30%.
