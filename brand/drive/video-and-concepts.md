## 0. READ FIRST: current facts override older briefs

Source: 00_BLISSTA_FACTS.md (Hemant, v1.0, 15 Sep 2026) plus the Teardown SOP. Where an older brief disagrees, these win.

- **Guarantee = 60 days on every SKU, keep the bottle.** Briefs from Apr to Jul 2026 say 120 days, and a few say 90. Rewrite every such line to 60 days. Newer Corvael/Corbel docs already say 60 days, e.g. "even if it's empty" and "Send the empties back. Every dollar comes home."
- **Never name a country, state or city.** No "Made in Utah", no "Made in USA", no "Not China", no "one in Canada and one in Utah". Use "GMP-certified facility, third-party lab-tested, COA per batch". Hemant can lift this per batch; it was lifted for the MB gummy batch on 27 Aug 2026. "China" as the villain's origin still appears in the winning MB angle; get sign-off before naming any geography.
- No "FDA-approved". Don't say "Sugar Free" on the gummies.
- **Product specs:**
  - MB liquid: 1% (10 mg/mL), 30 mL, 20 mg vitamin C per serving, USP, no formaldehyde or alcohol. Older copy: "1 drop = 1 mg".
  - MB gummies: briefs say 7 mg per gummy, 2 a day. The facts sheet says CONFIRM ON LABEL.
  - PainBloc: 600 mg micronized/ultramicronized PEA. One brief says 1,200 mg/day (FLAG).
  - Natto (Corvael, being renamed Corbel; the name is an open question): 20,000 FU NSK-SD per serving, enteric coated, NSF verified. The newest version is a 5-ingredient stack: 20,000 FU NSK-SD + K2 MK-7 200 mcg + aged garlic 600 mg + magnesium glycinate 200 mg + berberine 300 mg, 2 caps/day.
  - Aeon: 200 mcg liposomal GHK-Cu + 600 mg PEA + 120 mg HA + 250 mg vitamin C + 400 mg sodium bicarbonate.
  - Gluco Gone: no video briefs found.
- **Offer tiers:** Starter 1 bottle / 30 days; Most Popular 3 / 90 days (default, gold badge); Best Value 5–6 / 150 days. Subscription is about 25% off. The cancel-flow discount ceiling is 30%.
- **Offers seen in briefs:**
  - MB: Buy 3 get 2 free.
  - Corbel: 3 for the price of 2 plus free shipping; "order five, pay for three"; mystery bonus in the box.
  - Aeon: $34.99 / $79.99 (buy 2 get 1) / $119.99 (buy 3 get 2).
  - One MB brief: "First bottle's on us, you just cover shipping".
- **Never-cross lines:**
  - MB: no cancer, depression, Alzheimer's or COVID claims.
  - PainBloc: never say it "replaces gabapentin/Lyrica". Gabapentin is only "linked to / associated with" dementia.
  - Natto: no "blood thinner", "clot dissolver" or "stroke prevention". Never replaces warfarin/Eliquis/Xarelto. Never "lowers BP", "clears arteries" or "dissolves plaque".
  - Aeon: no hormone or cognitive claims. On screen, say "Restoration Tripeptide", not "peptide" (Meta flag).
  - Gluco Gone: no diabetes, A1C or metformin claims.
  - All products: no cure/treat language and no replacing meds. The Teardown SOP says never write "talk to your doctor"-style hedges as filler.

### Editor process (SOP_1_VIDEO_EDITOR)

- **Volume and pay:** 50 concepts/month, paid at 25 and at 50. A variation of a winner counts double.
- **Folders:** `01 EDITORS/<name>/YYYY-MM/YYYY-MM-DD C## <name>/hook01.mp4 ...`, plus a monthly `_CLIPS` folder.
- **Clip naming:** `PRODUCT_SCENE_DESCRIPTION_STATUS`.
  - Products: MB / PEA / COR / AEON / GLUCO.
  - Scenes: mechanism / problem / proof / product / person / environment.
  - New clips are always marked UNTESTED.
- **Clip library:** search 02 CLIP LIBRARY (PROVEN) before generating anything new.
- **Messages:** one concept per message. Revisions come before new work.

### Editor SOP (04_EDITOR_SOP v3)

**The five reasons editors get send-backs:**
1. Random B-roll
2. Cutting too fast
3. Visuals that don't match the line
4. Hard cuts
5. Doing only the literal brief

**Rules:**
- B-roll matches the exact sentence being spoken. "Every claim gets a matching visual. Generic B-roll = fail."
- At least 1.5–2 s per meaningful shot.
- Emotion matches the script. Transitions are smooth.
- Casting fits the avatar.
- Match the reference ad 1:1.

**The 11 brief rules:**
1. Script is verbatim.
2. Hooks change only the entry.
3. [Brackets] are facts to confirm.
4. Real people stay real.
5. Never put two review subjects in the same room or clothing.
6. No speed-ups.
7. No added music.
8. No Foley on the sung format.
9. Don't trim silence.
10. Don't fix artifacts; flag them.
11. Don't polish.

**Brief layout:** READ FIRST / BEAT MAP / NEVER CUT / CONFIRM BEFORE SHIP.
- Example NEVER CUT: Corbel song, bars 27/31, tight B/C shot, no B-roll. "That beat is the absolution."

**AI production:** order is voice, then characters, then shots, all on the same seed. Matched pairs are generated once and rendered twice. Some items require asking first (ask-before list).

**Delivery:**
- All 6 hooks at once, on one link, with variations.
- **File name = brief name.** Lesson: 33 ads went out named "NATTO-NATTO-images - Copy" and could not be read in reporting.
- 14-item export checklist (captions safe zone, 9:16, loudness, hook files separate, etc.).

## 2. Winners and performance data

Source: 01_WINNERS plus metrics quoted inside briefs.

**Proven winners (01_WINNERS):**

1. **SUMO WRESTLER (Corvael):** 51% hook / 45% hold / 8% CTR / $40–50 CPA / $10k/day. "This one is fake... Chinese soybean dust. They call it nattokinase. Your wife paid forty dollars for it." An iteration later ran at 56% hook / 3.84% hold.
   - The **wife-buyer version is unbuilt**. Youssef T37 drafts it: "If you bought your husband nattokinase and he felt nothing, this is for you."
2. **PAIN NURSE (PainBloc):** 5% CTR, $0.65 CPC. "I'm a pain nurse. This is what I use instead of gabapentin." The current never-cross rule bans "replaces gabapentin"; keep it as a personal choice, not a claim.
3. **CHINESE CONTAMINATION (MB):** $4M+ profitable spend. "Your MB is from China. All of it." Proof device: blue pee at 14 h.
   - Scaled through **format variations on ONE angle**: HM-AD-1 mini-VSL (4:35, 47.24% hold, $79 CPA, the highest hold in the account), "Mom's Kitchen Table" Confessor, TY-AD-1 clone, water test.
4. **TIER LIST (Corvael):** about 50% hook, 17% hold, 7% CTR, but a bad CPA. The guarantee is stated 3 ways.
5. **SIX WEEKS (Corvael):** "Six weeks on nattokinase and your BP didn't drop. That's the tell. It wasn't your body." Described as "absolutely crushing" on 2 Sep 2026. (BP wording: frame it as what the buyer expected, never as a claim.)

**Four things in every winner:**
1. Explicit absolution: "It wasn't your body / you didn't fail it."
2. A concession, e.g. "some of them are real, I'll give you that".
3. Self-verification: turn the bottle over, plate test, pee test, water test.
4. The same short check set.

**Losers and lessons:**
- The 33 mis-named ads.
- "Barbara" AI avatar.
- BP-numbers ad (under 150).
- Withholding information after showing a number.
- Same-room UGC.
- Personal cognitive-decline stories lost to clinical data.
- Zone 1–2 soft hooks (17–30% hook rate).
- Practitioner voice to a cold audience.
- Demographic and format multipliers: MB statics 0.72x, female MB audience 0.77x, 18–24 MB audience 0.48x.
- **Open question:** "The Screaming" / "The Drug Sentence" (female fibro) has never run.

**Other numbers in briefs:**

  - $99.39 CPA / 0.72 ROAS / 35.34% hook / 39.17% hold / AOV $72.04.
