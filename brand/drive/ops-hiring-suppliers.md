## 1. HIRING (05_HIRING_SOP, last change 30 Sep 2026, Hemant)
**Principle:** hire on paid work on the real product, not on CVs or interviews. Funnel: scorecard → post → application (Loom, except editors) → 15-min call → paid trial → decide. Never decide on the call. Close the call with "I've got a paid trial, details today."

**Scorecard (write before the post):** a one-line Mission. 3-8 ranked Outcomes, written as outcomes and not activities (e.g. "18 editors deliver on time without founder chasing"). Three competencies for every seat: follows up unasked, says when something is wrong, doesn't need a task list.

**Job post:** the title is the first filter (Head of Ops ≠ Ops Manager, AI Video Editor ≠ Video Editor). Open with the real problem. Include the outcomes word for word, a WHO THIS ISN'T section ("not BPO/VA, not for someone who needs a task list"), and the money: offshore roles show salary in local currency at the top, about 3x the local average. Creative roles show upside instead of a number (base + uncapped commission tied to spend).

**Channels:** OnlineJobs.ph (post under Management, not Admin; max 3 skill tags) and Indeed PH (salary in pesos up top) for ops, support, editors and designers. Paid LinkedIn for senior roles (free posts get buried). FB VA groups for editors and designers. DTC communities (eCommerceFuel, 2PM, Slack/Discord) for strategists. Indeed and free LinkedIn **failed** for the copywriter and strategist searches. Post free in your own groups first.
**Headhunting** is for senior creative. Sources: Meta Ad Library (who made the ads you admire), referrals from own contractors (highest yield), DTC community posters, LinkedIn strategists about 18 months into a role. DM template: name the specific work, lead with scale ("supplement brand, multi-eight figures on Meta, 100-300 concepts/week"). 10 DMs gets about 3 replies and 2 worth testing, which equals the yield of a 50-applicant post.
**Geo cost/month:** India/Pakistan $1,000-1,800 (written English is a risk for customer-facing work). Philippines $1,500-3,000 (best execution layer). South Africa $2,500-4,000 (best judgement; pay the premium where judgement matters).

**Application:** no CV, ever. Creative seats send a 1-5 min Loom: "Show work that ran on paid traffic, what was YOUR part, and what it did (spend/ROAS)". Ignoring the Loom instruction is itself a fail. **Video editors: no Loom since 30 Sep 2026.** Review their submitted work (priority: DR, long-form, AI-generated/animation, talking head, VO+B-roll, paid social), then call, then paid trial. No unpaid assignments.
Ops/management seats answer 4 questions: (1) a company you ran ops for: size and what you owned; (2) a time you noticed something wrong first; (3) walk through a process you own: inputs, steps, outputs, exceptions (the sharpest question); (4) how you'd track 18 editors without daily check-ins.

**Loom read. Green:** real numbers (ROAS/CTR, not reach), volunteers a failure, names tools unprompted (Nano Banana, Higgsfield, TrendTrack, ElevenLabs), explains why, small specific examples, names people, proposes a system. **Red:** a teardown of someone else's ad, no numbers, reach presented as performance, wants to own the concept when the job is execution, answers with philosophy, asks zero questions. Editor reds: work not theirs, can't explain their edit, below standard, can't run from a brief, poor DR pacing.

**Call (15 min), three questions:** (1) Execute a brief, or own the concept? (decides most creative hires). (2) Five-bosses: "how will each of your last 5 bosses rate you 1-10 when I call?" (3) What can you realistically deliver in a month?
Live tests use real account material only: strategist (play 2 of our ads, break both down, which scaled past $100k, fix the loser); copywriter (rewrite a hook live, six doors); editor (open a real brief: what first, what to ask); media buyer (real ad set at $150 spend: your call); CS (3 real messages, one of them a blood-thinner question).

Trial rules: same task for everyone; candidates use their own tools (paid for if hired); don't tell them they're competing; "if you'll be short, say so before end of day". Write a week-by-week (W1-W4) expectations sheet with specific outputs before any non-editor trial starts. The cause of month-1 quits was unwritten expectations.

**Scoring rubric (score while reviewing, 1 line of evidence per score):**
- Creative: Proof / Tools / Brief-taking / Volume.
- Editor screen: same four. Editor trial: Brief comprehension / DR judgement / Editing quality / AI execution / Problem solving / Reliability.
- Ops: Initiative / Scale / Process / Honesty.
- Gates: ≤3 on Proof or Initiative means no trial. 4+ across the board goes to a call. For editors the paid trial is the deciding evidence.

**Money:** order is trial fee → base → review → performance. Never agree a base before seeing output. Set the "good" line before the trial ends. Pre-start negotiation script: "base is between X and Y, not decided until I see you work."
Editors/designers bonus ladder on profitable spend: $1k/$2k/$3k/$5k at $50k/$150k/$300k/$500k, capped per ad, paid at the highest tier (not stacked). Strategists: base or flat fee + % of spend behind their concepts at a ROAS floor. A "winner" is a top spender holding the floor; attribute at concept level.
**Contract:** a one-page plain version (Scope, Payment, Bonus, Review, Other). Permanent hires get work-for-hire, IP assignment, 14-day notice with forfeiture, 12-month non-solicit, Wyoming law.
**Onboarding:** add to #blissta-announcements, share the BLISSTA OPERATIONS folders per the Access Map (they read START HERE), announce publicly ("X runs Y from Monday, go to them first"), name one person for the work and one for the product.
**One thing:** hire the one who noticed something, not the most polished.

## 5. SUPPORT SOPs (06_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY v2.0; CS_Refund_Playbook; Guarantee_Fix_Checklist)
- **Stack:** Meta ad → advertorial → CheckoutChamp funnel (try.blissta.co) → upsell → Shopify (custom line items) → Klaviyo → CC rebills → members.blissta.co/portal. **CheckoutChamp is the system of record for money. Refund in CC only.** A Shopify refund is a "ghost refund" and leads to a chargeback.
- **Morning job, first:** check every Shopify order over $100. A recurring order at high value is the overcharge bug (rebill at full list price, e.g. $95.98 paid → $439 billed). Fix: CC → Customers (6-month range) → **Continuity** tab (not Orders) → set the price actually paid and **interval = qty × 28 days** (1 = 28, 3 = 84, 5 = 140; 42/44/48/70 are wrong) → **UPDATE PURCHASE** → refund the overcharge in Orders → send the apology email whether or not they complained.
- **Declined-card retry, daily:** CC Reports → Sales → Continuity, filter Last Declined, Recycle Failed, work backwards day by day. Use **one gateway per day** (MyCPO / PayArc / EMS-Kurv, rotate). Check the amount before toggling. Fix 5-packs individually. 64.8% of post-first-order churn is involuntary.
- **Checkout overcharge:** $150-200 orders with 8+ items mean the customer picked a bundle and set qty 5. Fix in Continuity, partial refund, email.
- **Refund playbook (orders from 17 Sep 2026):** 30 days from delivery, no questions, no return, product price only, one per customer (matched by name, email, address or card), within 24h Mon-Fri. Five checks: delivered ≤30d, email matches, bought on blissta.co (not Amazon), no prior refund, no open chargeback.
- **Refund exceptions:** charged after cancel or unauthorized charge → full refund including shipping, doesn't count as their one refund. Damaged → photo within 7 days. Stuck 10+ business days → reship or refund. Delivered but missing → wait 48h, then carrier claim within 7 days. Bank/chargeback threat with nothing filed → refund within the hour. Chargeback already filed → do NOT refund; tag Bruce and pull the evidence pack (order + IP, T&C acceptance, subscription consent and reminders, tracking, messages, policy screenshot, prior refund). Never say a refund is done before it is done in CC (the #1 chargeback cause). Templates A-E exist.
- **Cancel flow:** pause first. Reason routes the offer. Too Expensive → +30% off (testing). Didn't Know I Subscribed → cancel, refund, no offer. Never automate more than 30% off. Loop's 20% blanket discount got about 1% uptake. Always log a reason.
- **Autoresponders:** Pre-Billing 3 days before rebill (the "most important email"). Recurring Order Declined.
- **Seven leaks:** PayPal logo shown while PayPal is disabled (23 conversations). Guarantee numbers (30/60/120). Ad $87.99 vs page $97.99 vs cart $400+. Free-shipping claim (fixed: bundle 3 only). Unclear auto-refill. Wrong SKU shipped. China parcels under a Made in USA claim.
- **Guarantee fix checklist:** a site-wide list of every 120/90/60-day mention to change to "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, keep the bottle, refund within 24h". Covers Shopify theme checkout text (7 spots), the FAQ page, product templates (methlenblue, mb-gummies, mb-caps, pea, restoration-tripeptide-co, neto-101, t-l-b-150-only, metheloate, beet-root), the homepage, collections, subscription pages, about 15 Replo pages (edit in Replo, not Shopify code), then ads, Klaviyo, CC offer pages, Amazon listings and inserts. Cleanup: 39 unused templates, hidden sections, the empty Our Story page, and a Judge.me spam review making an HSV cure claim. Never write "return", "send it back", "unused" or "original packaging".

## 7. MEDIA BUYING (08_MEDIA_BUYING_SOP v3.0; 08_HOW_WE_TEST)
- Hemant makes every kill and scale call. Uploader [NAME] is TBD. Tool: Ad Uploader ($59/mo) pulls from the BlisstaUSA1 Google Drive; 165 ads per upload is normal. Order: create the campaign + 1 ad set in Ads Manager and publish, then upload in Ad Uploader. Settings: Sales, Website, pixel, **Maximum number of conversions (not value)**, Purchase, $100/day, top 5 countries. 7-8 hooks per AVO concept in one ad set; 14-16 for offer ads; 5 primary copies. NY time.
- **Ladder:** ABO $100/day → kill at $150 with 0 sales; 1-2 sales at breakeven → run to $250; still not profitable → kill. Every kill goes to a cost cap campaign ($70-80), then bid cap ($90-100), then dead. About 1/3 of ABO deaths win on cost cap. CPA ceiling $100. Break-even ROAS 1.2 (1.1-1.15 tolerated on $100k+ concepts). Gates are spend, not days; old day-3/day-7 rules are dead.
- **Scaling:** pull ads with more than 5 sales (last 7 or 30 days) and duplicate them by existing post ID into "USA Net High Volume" (US only, max conversions) and "US Net" (US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/SG/Europe minus Russia, bid cap $120-130). Ad set "ALL WINNERS AVO [DATE]", a new one monthly. Expect 40-60 winners per 30-day sweep.
- **Testing rules:** isolate one variable. Mix 70% winner variations / 20% one-variable iterations / 10% wild cards. 5-7 ad sets (max 10), one angle per ad set. Judge by awareness level: problem-aware on hook/hold, solution-aware on hold/CTR, most-aware on CPA/ROAS. Hook rate under 25% is dead. Benchmarks: Sumo Wrestler 51% hook / 45% hold / 8% CTR. Static validates, native scales. Don't start a new concept before 200+ days and $300K+ on the current one. Anti-repetition gate: mechanism, persona, story, register; at most 5 concepts per session. Every concept needs a hypothesis with predicted hook and hold.
- **Diagnostics:** good hook + good CTR + bad sales means a hook/body mismatch, so it goes back to the strategist. Everything good but a bad CPA means a post-click leak (see the 06 leaks). Attribution 7d click / 1d engaged view. Exclude 180-day purchasers on every campaign. Clikim ad accounts get disabled without warning.
- **Naming:** `TEST | PRODUCT | MONTH`, `SCALE US|T5 | PRODUCT | MONTH WINNERS`, `CC | PRODUCT`, `BC | PRODUCT`. Ad name = brief file name (lesson from 33 ads all named "NATTO-NATTO-images - Copy"). Log every ad: date, concept, format, prediction, spend, sales, CPA, hook, hold, lesson.
