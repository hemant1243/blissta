## Q1. What do Blissta's winning statics look like, and which visual patterns repeat? (mapped to awareness level and message type)

### Takeaway
The 31 filed "winning" statics (Jul 2026, BLISSTA - WINNING ADS / IMAGE folders) cluster into about nine repeatable devices: label forensic with a red circle, 5-bottle fan + kraft "ENDS SUNDAY" tag + gold pill, cream-heritage "doctor" frame, itemized receipt with strikethroughs, absorption/before-after split, ingredient grid/checklist, protocol hero, fake-document "alert/audit", and no-text native evidence photos. They share big 2-line ALL-CAPS headlines (heavy serif for trust, heavy condensed sans for offers), an italic serif subline carrying the specifics, an offer/guarantee footer bar, and the navy-and-gold Corvael bottle either centre-forward (offers) or as a bottom-right inset (proof ads). Caveat: the folder holds no metrics or stated reasons for winning, and many of these break today's compliance rules.

### Inferences
- The visually and strategically strongest repeatable devices are the ones that make the "four things every winner does" visible: absolution + self-check (label forensic, absorption split, receipt), concession/safety (doctor frame), then offer devices for hot traffic. The offer-fan family (NATTO 13/14/17, GUMMIES 1, MB DROPS) is the most consistent visual system (dark bg + warm glow + fanned product + gold/kraft tag + pill).
- Awareness mapping (my inference, not stated in sources): evidence photos = unaware/problem-aware; label forensic, absorption split, 5-reasons grid, audit tables = problem/solution-aware failed users (belief A/B in [EBM]); doctor frame, protocol, ritual = product-aware; receipt, 5-bottle fan, price card, MB drops = most-aware/retargeting. This lines up with the SOP's offer-by-temperature rule (cold = no offer, warm = guarantee/price per day, hot = full stack).
- Winning statics in the folder are older (pre-Sep 14) and stylistically closer to the SOP v2.0 systems (cream heritage, navy/gold, black/amber) than to the Oct 8 v2.1 system (scene backgrounds, sentence case, red/green accents). No evidence yet that v2.1-style statics have won; they were only being published Oct 8-15.

## Q2. What do the SOPs and briefs require (backgrounds, colour systems, fonts, sizes, text limits, the 15 killers, Magnific settings, prompt templates, porting)?

### Takeaway
There are now four layers of rules: Static SOP v2.0 (Sep 14: 15 concepts × 3 hooks, three background systems, 11 laws, 15 killers), the Designer Brief (Sep 30: port one TrendTrack winner into a 1:1 + 4 sub-avatar variants, Magnific/Nano Banana settings, a 10-block prompt template, navy/gold/white), the Ad Factory SOP (Oct 14: Claude-driven pipeline, 4:5, six proven templates), and the newest Slack canvas Design System v2.1 (Oct 8), which explicitly overrides earlier decision rules with scene backgrounds, a near-black/red #E0261B/green #1E7B34 accent "house look", one heavy sans family in sentence case, product at 30–40% of height, and a nine-item kill list.

### Cited Findings
**Static SOP v2.0 (owner Hemant, 14 Sep 2026)**
- Daily target 15 concepts / 45 images (one base render per concept, 3 headline overlays); concept = "one argument, one visual idea, one thing on screen"; 15 fixed concept slots (wrong belief, reframe, failed solution, dismissal, the number, comparison, physical proof, villain in one word, mechanism as one image, daily loss, relationship beat, scarcity, guarantee, offer, pub line) — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md); [SOP v2.0](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ma_yfi_oIYHNzs3MhfHIFLkEBOn2gD9F/view)
- Three hooks per concept with three different triggers (absolution, reframe, fear/loss, curiosity gap, social proof, identity, offer); lock concept/visual/background and change only the headline — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Render prompt structure: `[SUBJECT] [SETTING] [LIGHTING] [CAMERA] [COLOUR hex] [COMPOSITION focal point/product placement/negative space] [STYLE photoreal/3D/native phone] [NEGATIVE no text, no watermark, no hands unless specified]`; "Give hex codes, not 'cream'"; "Write the hook first, then find the photo that proves it." — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Three background systems: (1) Clean clinical: white/near-white, black headlines, one accent — evidence, label comparisons, checklists; (2) Cream heritage `#F4ECDC · #3A2818 · #C89D4E` — Japanese authority, testimonials, before/after, "old and proven"; (3) Vascular oxblood `#4A0F0F · #E8A82C` + cream cards — splits, cinematic premium, urgency, stakes. Break the system only when the format depends on its own look (Reddit must look like Reddit; "an 'ugly green Facebook native' must look ugly"), justified in one sentence — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- "No red, no blood, no gore in artery renders. Warm cream and dusty rose." — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Density: Minimal = hook only; Medium = hook + subline; Heavy = hook, subline, benefits, proof, offer; test density as its own variable. Offer by temperature: cold usually none, warm guarantee or price/day, hot full stack — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Delivery: `[PRODUCT]_[CONCEPT]_H[1/2/3]_[RATIO].jpg`; 4:5 default, JPG, sRGB, under 2MB — [SADS §1A](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Laws: under 10 words default, 3–7 words do the work; one message/one visual/one action; specificity; native beats polished; visual amplifies never competes ("Show the actual thing: the label, the artery, the banana"); one focal point, eye locks in under 0.4 s; his exact words; reframe before you sell; "He will Google your claim"; "If you're not sure, cut something." — [SADS §2](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- The 15 killers (any one = does not ship): 1 can't tell what it says in 2 s; 2 two focal points; 3 visual doesn't match headline; 4 marketer language; 5 claim with no number/name/date; 6 number not in BLISSTA_FACTS; 7 low contrast (grey on grey, thin fonts, script fonts); 8 three benefits instead of one message; 9 it looks like an ad; 10 heavy fear with no way out; 11 background outside the 3 systems, unjustified; 12 stock photography any brand could own; 13 three hooks with same trigger; 14 disease or medication-replacement claim; 15 country-of-origin language while open — [SADS §4](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)

**Designer Brief (Sep 30, BF/VIP offer work)**
- Every static starts from a proven TrendTrack winner (Active, sort by reach/scaling, last quarter, Image media; confirm in Ad Library by impressions); 6-question breakdown (concept in one sentence, eye path 1st/2nd/3rd, offer device, headline job, scroll stopper, what stays 1:1); write one 1:1 + 4 variants and get approval before any prompts — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md); [BRIEF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DMzgDAyzcrCE2Ibj25NEXTQngzQX9l87/view)
- Variants V2–V5 keep the layout and swap headline + background per sub-avatar: deal hunter = kitchen counter at dawn/coffee/pill organizer; active 55+ = back porch golden hour; skeptical researcher = dark lab bench/cool light; anti-establishment insider = dark table, one lamp, black envelope with gold VIP card — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Magnific settings "every time": Google Nano Banana 2, aspect ratio 1:1 set in Magnific, Quality 1K High, Thinking High, 1 image per gen; upload real product shots and reference "@img1"; don't attach the competitor ad, describe it — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Prompt rules: never "same layout"; describe position, size (% of canvas), hex, font style, lighting, props; text in quotes + "Render only the text in quotes, spelled exactly"; full-bleed line "No borders, no frame, no white margins, no poster inside a poster"; labels "Copy each bottle exactly… Do not redraw, blur or invent label text"; no stars, ratings, extra logos, watermark — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Prompt template blocks: STYLE · CANVAS · REFERENCES · BACKGROUND (incl. "where it goes dark for text contrast") · HEADLINE (position, font, hex, size %) · OFFER LINE · PRODUCT · TAGS/BADGES · CTA + BOTTOM BAR · LIGHTING ("Photographic, not illustrated") · TEXT RULES — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Good-prompt example specifies hexes: sunburst `#F2B24C → #C9731E`, gold sticker `#E2B24A` rotated 10°, "dark navy extra heavy italic text" — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Slop list: split layout half stock photo/half text; random AI couple in a kitchen; product tiny or labels cut off; every line same weight; made-up prices or mismatched %; headline with no offer; off-brand colours (maroon). Good = proven pattern kept; order "headline → offer → proof → product → button, readable in 2 s"; offer impossible to miss; real prices/deadline; background talks to one sub-avatar — [SADS §1B](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Pre-send checklist includes full bleed, aspect ratio set in Magnific, labels readable and matching, prices match offer table, biggest bundle pushed, no Trustpilot/fake stars/"Made in USA" on Corbel, nothing physically fake (no liquid from capsule bottles), "Navy, gold, white (unless the winner used otherwise)" — [SADS §4](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
- Buyer: 55+, mostly men, "skeptical as hell", zooms in on labels, does the math, hates hype; "Never make him look old, sad or broken." — [SADS §2](/home/user/blissta/brand/drive/static-ads-and-sops.md)
