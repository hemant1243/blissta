### 4.7 The creative process, step by step (as he runs it)

Step 1. Read the rules. Every chat starts with BLISSTA_OPERATING_RULES.md ("Read this first, every chat"), plus THE_METHOD_Universal.md and the relevant avatar map. Strategist Bible and Villain Playbook are "read every Monday."

Step 2. Research before writing, every time. "SEARCH BEFORE YOU WRITE. EVERY TIME." Sources in order: project knowledge → past conversations → own ad comments (Commslayer, Drive exports) → competitor ad comments → Reddit / Amazon / YouTube comments / FB groups / WebMD / Drugs.com. Build a verbatim language bank sorted by emotional state, never paraphrased. "NEVER use AI for customer research." "1,000+ exact verbatim quotes per major project." Adlicio and Trendtrack are the tools.

Step 3. Pick the avatar by the Evolve method. Five categories in power order: Desire, Experience (situational or product based), Emotion, Behaviour, Demographics. Core avatar is ONE category. Sub avatar is core plus layers. "YOU NEVER WRITE AN AD TO A CORE AVATAR." Build on the surface desire ("I want to stop needing a nap at 2pm"), never the core desire ("I want to feel like myself again"). State it as: "Core: Experience, product based, bought a nattokinase and it turned out to be worthless. Layers: Emotion (anger at being conned) + Experience (his wife bought it for him)." Never ask age, gender, or condition. On ad teardown ports, always ask which product the port is for even if the teardown names one (his standing instruction).

Step 4. Find winners to rip (Trendtrack SOP, Sept 2026). Go in through the shop domain, not the brand name (brands run natives on persona pages). Filter: Status Active; Media Image; creation date last 30 to 35 days; days running min 7; growth Rising; copy length min 1500 chars; hide low impression; country / language / niche off. Pull twice: sort by Spend/Impressions desc, then Ad Rank asc with top 3 percent filter; dedupe. "Do not scan. The full ad body comes back inside the search results. Scans are for video transcripts only." Budget 40 credits per brand. Validation gates (Ranking System): active; 7+ days; rising; rank 40 or better; not low impression; 1500+ chars; not already ported. Rank tiers: same copy on two or more pages beats two live copies on one page beats one live copy. "An ad with 3 or more live copies goes on the list even if its rank fails." Hand back one ad at a time; "Do not start three teardowns at once."
